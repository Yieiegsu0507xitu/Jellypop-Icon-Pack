Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JellyPop handles information when you use the JellyPop icon pack application.

We respect your privacy and have designed JellyPop so that the application does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics, or tracking purposes.

1. Information We Collect

JellyPop does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or password to use the application.

JellyPop does not use advertising services, analytics services, or tracking technologies to collect information about your activity within the application.

2. Icon Pack and Wallpapers

JellyPop provides icons, wallpapers, and customization content as part of the application. These features are intended to function as part of the icon pack and do not require JellyPop to collect personal information from you.

3. Kustom Widgets

JellyPop may provide Kustom widget presets or customization resources. These resources are provided for customization purposes and JellyPop does not use them to collect personal information.

4. Premium Icon Requests

JellyPop may provide an optional premium icon request feature. If you choose to submit a premium icon request, information required to process the request may be included with the request, such as device information, Android version, application version, requested application details, and the transaction information associated with the premium request.

This information is used only for the purpose of processing and fulfilling the requested icon service and is not used for advertising or behavioral tracking.

5. Payments

Payments for JellyPop or its premium features are processed through Google Play. JellyPop does not directly collect or store your payment card, bank account, or other payment credentials.

Payment information is handled by Google Play according to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

6. Third-Party Services

JellyPop may rely on Google Play services for application distribution and payment processing. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

JellyPop does not sell, rent, or trade personal information with third parties.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection and handling of personal information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

JellyPop does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the application does not require an account or personal information for normal use, there is no intentional collection of children's personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to JellyPop or its features. Any changes will be published on this page, along with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of JellyPop, please contact the JellyPop developer through the contact information provided on the application's official store listing.

11. Your Consent

By installing or using JellyPop, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
